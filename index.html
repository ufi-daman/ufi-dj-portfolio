About

UFI DA MAN

UFI DA MAN is a Prague-based DJ and producer constantly moving on the edge of the underground and the mainstream scene. He first found his relationship with electronic music in 1993, when he started experimenting with early Tracker software.

Over three decades his sound evolved through electro, house, techno, nu-rave and nu-disco — shaped by countless nights at Prague's iconic clubs and open-air stages. Working in FL Studio and Ableton, he crafts sets and originals that hit hard and stay with you long after the lights come on.
